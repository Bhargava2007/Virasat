Heritage

Parasuramesvara Temple
The best-preserved and earliest surviving temple of Bhubaneswar (c. 650 CE), marking the beginning of the Kalinga temple-building tradition.
Konark Sun Temple
A 13th-century UNESCO World Heritage Site built in the form of a colossal chariot, adorned with exquisite stone carvings of celestial beings and erotic sculptures.
Mukteshvara Temple
Known as the gem of Odishan architecture, this 10th-century temple is celebrated for its ornate torana gateway and intricate sculptural reliefs.
Lingaraja Temple
The largest temple in Bhubaneswar, dedicated to Harihara — a fusion of Shiva and Vishnu — and a defining example of the Kalinga style at its zenith.
